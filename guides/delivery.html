Delivery & service area

You found the appliance. Now let's get it to your house. Delivery is simple: $50 flat, delivered to your door anywhere in Bloomington-Normal, IL.

How delivery works

  1. Pick your appliance — browse the catalog and text or call to claim it.
  2. Schedule a time — we'll find a delivery window that works for you.
  3. We bring it to your door — $50 flat within Bloomington-Normal, IL.

Outside Bloomington-Normal?

No problem — we deliver to surrounding communities all the time. Text or call for a quote; the price depends on distance. (Delivery costs move with fuel prices, so we quote each trip individually rather than posting a rate that goes stale.)

Installation services

Need it hooked up, not just dropped off? Installation services are available for an additional charge — dishwashers, ranges, over-the-range microwaves, and more. Text for pricing; we'll tell you straight whether your setup is simple or complicated before you commit to anything.

Prefer to pick up?

You're welcome to come see everything in person first — the warehouse is open by appointment. Call or text to schedule a time, and we'll have the unit pulled out and ready when you arrive.
